Incident report, October

Platform team. Prepared for the monthly service review on 4 November.

Period: 1 to 31 October. Times are in UTC.

Six incidents affected customers in October. Four came from changes that we deployed. Two came from a provider. The table gives the start, the cause and the fix of each incident.

Summary

  • 6 incidents, 218 minutes of downtime in total.
  • 4 incidents came from our own changes.
  • The longest incident stopped checkout for 63 minutes.
  • No data was lost.

Incidents

IDWhat happenedMinutes
INC-101The login page returned errors for users in one region. A new load balancer rule sent their requests to a server pool that was not ready. We removed the rule and added a health check to the deploy script. Closed 3 October.42
INC-102PDF invoices were empty for 40 minutes. The renderer started before the fonts were installed in the new image, so every page had no text. We pinned the image, added a font check to the start script and sent the affected invoices again. Closed 7 October.40
INC-103The DNS provider had an outage. Requests to the API failed for clients that do not retry. We added a second DNS provider and asked the two largest clients to turn on retries. Closed 9 October.25
INC-104A database migration locked the orders table. Checkout waited for the lock and timed out. The migration ran in the day, not in the maintenance window, because a scheduled job used the wrong time zone. We moved the job to UTC, split the migration into small batches and added an alert for long locks. Customers who could not pay received an email with a link to finish the order. The finance team compared the orders with the payments on the next day. Closed 12 October.63
INC-105The email provider delayed password reset messages by up to 30 minutes. We showed a notice on the reset page until the queue was empty. Closed 18 October.30
INC-106A disk on the search server became full because log rotation stopped after an upgrade. Search returned errors until we deleted the old logs. We added a disk usage alert at 80 percent. Closed 24 October.18
Total downtime218

Causes

Two of the six incidents came from scheduled jobs that ran at the wrong time. Both jobs used the local time of the server, and one server changed its time zone during the upgrade in September.

One incident came from a missing font in a new image. The image build did not check the fonts, and the tests used a page without text.

The other three incidents had different causes: a load balancer rule, a DNS outage and a full disk.

Customer impact

The total downtime was 218 minutes. In September it was 95 minutes. Checkout was not available for 63 minutes, and 41 customers could not pay. All of them received an email with a link to finish the order.

All incidents have a written review. The reviews are in the team wiki.

The service review on 4 November discussed each incident with the team leads. The meeting agreed on four actions. Each action has an owner and a date in the team plan. The next report shows the status of each action and the incidents of November. Send questions about this report to the platform team.

The plan is in the shared drive. The team leads review it every Monday. Each owner reports progress in the weekly call. The plan also lists the incidents that need a second review with the provider.

Next steps

  • Move the backup job and all other scheduled jobs to UTC.
  • Run database migrations only in the maintenance window.
  • Add a font check to the image build for the PDF renderer.
  • Test the second DNS provider once a month.
